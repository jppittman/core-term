# The class-cap sweep

Every row is one kernel compiled through the production path's three calls with the production optimizer held to `--class-cap N --app-cap M` (classical's 100 rounds; `Budget::Explicit`). Saturation columns are the optimizer's own `saturation-telemetry` record for that compile, read back from stderr; `objective` is which extraction objective produced the term (`shared` / `tree_cheaper` both ran the sharing-aware pass; `tree_only` means it was abandoned at `SHARED_DAG_PASS_BYTE_BUDGET`). Deterministic columns are the claim; `compile ms` is `optimize_ms + emit_ms` under the counting allocator and is a sign at the stated load; `peak MB` is the peak net heap growth of the compile (`alloc_probe`).

INTERIM (14:10 PDT): arms 5,000 / 10,000 / 20,000 complete on every family; 50,000 and 100,000 complete on cellgrid, psychedelic and chrome, in flight on the glyphs and shaders. Host: aarch64 NEON, load 5-30 throughout (other sessions); compile ms is a sign, not a number.
